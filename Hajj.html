Hajj Days & Rituals (Kya Kya Karte Hain)

8 Dhul Hijjah (Day 1)
Mina Ka Safar
Hujjaj Ihram pehenkar Mina ke liye rawana hote hain aur wahan Zuhr, Asr, Maghrib, Isha, aur agle din ki Fajr ki namaz ada karte hain.
9 Dhul Hijjah (Day 2)
Arafat Ka Waqoof & Muzdalifah
Hajj ka sabse bada rukan. Arafat ke maidan mein Wuqoof aur dua ki jaati hai. Maghrib ke baad bina namaz padhe Muzdalifah jana hota hai aur wahan Maghrib+Isha milakar padhte hain aur khule aasman tale raat guzarte hain.
10 Dhul Hijjah (Day 3)
Rami, Qurbani, Aur Halq (Eid Day)
Mina laut kar bade Shaitan ko kankari marna (Rami), Qurbani karna, Baal katwana (Halq) aur Ihram kholna. Iske baad Makkah jakar Tawaf-e-Ziyarah kiya jata hai.
11 & 12 Dhul Hijjah (Day 4 & 5)
Ayyaam-e-Tashreeq
Mina mein qiyaam karna aur teeno Shaitano ko tarteeb se kankariyan marna. Hajj ke arkaan mukammal karne ki khushi aur shukrana.

Hajj Ki Taiyari

  • Niyyat aur Ihram ke masail seekhna.
  • Kasrat se Talbiyah padhna.
  • Sabr aur husn-e-akhlaq ka muzahira karna.
  • Zaroori duayein yaad karna.
